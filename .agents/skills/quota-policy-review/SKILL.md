# Official regulatory review

Prefer European Commission quota consultation/TARIC, EUR-Lex, GOV.UK implementation and tariff guidance, and official UK quota CSV. News reports can identify changes but do not establish legal effect.

Capture exact legal instrument, publication date, entry-into-force date, steel category, CN/HS codes, origin, quota order number, applicable period and status (proposal/consultation/adopted/in force). Distinguish rules of allocation from balance reporting and trade-remedy duties outside the quota.

For China-origin EU products, verify China-specific order numbers and eligibility for Other Countries pools rather than presuming availability. Never equate a positive residual balance with guaranteed duty-free clearance. Opening-quarter proportional allocation must have historical evidence tied to declaration-acceptance date.

For UK, verify residual pool, carryover, blocking/critical states and implementation timing. Public fill rate does not provide same-day allocation percentages.

Produce a cited review note and regression fixtures before any code adjustment. Require human review of policy-sensitive changes even when checks pass.
